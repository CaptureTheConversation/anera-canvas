01
Product / Brand

What Anera Collective is

A boutique consultancy for mission-driven founders who want to run their company on a regenerative operating model instead of an extractive one.

Offerings (three, one operating model)
  • Executive coaching - founder and executive-level, Harrison Assessments certified.
  • Fractional Chief People Officer engagements - the Executive-in-a-Box stance, embedded across people, culture, and business strategy.
  • Eventing - keynote speaking and offsite facilitation that moves whole teams through the framework live.
Author-founder
  • Sarah Pearson. Grew the company that became Techstyle from 12 to 2,500 employees across 12 countries. Global head of talent for Fabletics, Savage X Fenty, JustFab, ShoeDazzle, FabKids.
  • Named after "a new era" - Anera is an era to be remembered, an era to see change.
02
Category Play: CREATE

Naming a new operating model, not a new HR firm.

FROM - the extractive default
  • HR is a compliance-and-people-operations function that supports the business.
  • Culture is a nice-to-have led by HR, owned by no one.
  • Scale first, care later, clean up the wreckage.
  • Extraction is neutral - just what scale takes.
→
TO - Anera's regenerative rules
  • People, planet, and profit are held in balance. Balance is what compounds.
  • The operating model is the culture. Culture is not the poster.
  • Cultivate the soil, then plant the season.
  • Care is not the cost of scale. Care is the strategy for scale.
Pressure-test
  • Regenerative Work Design as a whole-company operating model does not exist as a named category. Regenerative agriculture, regenerative business, and teal organizations are adjacent but different.
  • Anera does not need the whole market. The tribe already exists - B Corps, values-led CPG founders, teal-organization-inspired teams. They are looking for the language.
  • CREATE preserves the possibility of a collective and future practitioners inside the category Sarah authors.

Features & Capabilities

What we operate, not what we deliver.

  • The 20-Point HR Audit - the diagnostic entry point. Surfaces compliance exposure, systems gaps, and cultural risks. Feeds the roadmap.
  • Seasonal Mapping - Sarah's proprietary rhythm-mapping across spring (emergence), summer (expansion), autumn (release), winter (rest) at the level of a day, a week, a quarter, and a year. Individual, team, and company.
  • Executive-in-a-Box stance - Sarah operates as chief people officer, chief growth officer, and chief revenue officer scar tissue simultaneously. Thinks like a CEO, not just a CPO.
  • Harrison Assessments certification - enjoyment performance theory and paradox theory. The shared self-awareness layer that moves a team from a collection of individuals to a unit in synchronization.
  • Regenerative operating rituals - practices, cadences, and language patterns installed so the model runs after Sarah leaves.
  • Keynote and offsite facilitation - Sarah on stage or in the room, delivering the category story and moving teams through the framework live.
  • Twenty-five years of scale scar tissue - 12 to 2,500 employees across 12 countries. Fabletics, Savage X Fenty, JustFab, ShoeDazzle, FabKids. The landmines are already mapped.

Anti-ICP

Customer Ambition
"I am becoming the founder I set out to be."

Not "someone fixed my HR." The founder runs a company that is gentle to people and planet and highly profitable because of it, not despite it.

What it feels like
  • Peace of mind - the founder stops white-knuckling the company.
  • Team synchronization - the team moves from a collection of individuals to a unit working in season.
  • Culture that is legible instead of vibe-based, repeatable instead of accidental.
  • Best Places to Work as a downstream signal, not a KPI to chase.
  • Profit that follows care - and no longer feels like a trade-off with it.
  • Calm, seen, and held - the way you feel in nature.
08
The Enemy

The Extractive Operating Model.

The default way of running a company that treats people as capacity to be maximized, planet as raw material to be consumed, and profit as the only measurement that matters. Extraction was never neutral. It is now costing companies the very people, planet, and profit it promised to deliver.

Sub-villains
  • Grind culture - hustle as identity, burnout as badge.
  • HR-as-compliance - people operations reduced to paperwork and risk mitigation.
  • Culture-as-perks - snacks, swag, and off-sites masquerading as culture.
  • Scale-then-fix - the promise that you can grow first and repair the human wreckage later.
  • Extraction-neutral thinking - the belief that grinding people, land, and community is just the cost of business.
  • Toxic patriarchy and colonial defaults inside modern work - the operating structures we inherited and never questioned.
